# Provenance note: published Figure 6 context plot

`base-paper-fig6-replot.svg` is a redrawn, attributed context figure based on Figure 6 of Aslam, Bhardwaj, and Chaudhary, *Vehicular Communications* 52 (2025) 100880. The article does not make its underlying series available. The plotted line values were manually digitized from the published graphic, and are approximate; its stated endpoint values were retained where available.

This figure is for Related Work / motivation only. It must not be combined with this project's ML-DSA/ML-KEM measurements, presented as a reproduction, or used for a statistically significant comparison: the paper gives no seed count, dispersion, or confidence intervals for Figure 6.
